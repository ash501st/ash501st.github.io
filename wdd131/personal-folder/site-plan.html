Overview

Purpose

The purpose of this site is to help educate people about the importance of sharks in our ocean's ecosystem, the effects they have on our planet, how that effects you individually, and what you can do to help!

Audience

Everyone reading this is the intended audiance. Only with your awareness of this issue can we gain the help needed to make a difference!

Dynamic elements

I am planning on building an interactive application on the site using JavaScript to allow users to learn more about sharks.

Pseudocode for your project

For the "dynamic element", I am going to create a small "shark database", that has different classes of sharks listed, and an option for users to click one, and it will then display a random shark from the database that fits that subject, for example "Prehistoric sharks, deep sea sharks, apex predators, harmless to humans." I would also like it to give a small fact or something interesting about that shark.
